Room parent playbook

Things I've learned the hard way

📣 Ask early, ask twice

Send volunteer requests three weeks out, then a cheerful reminder one week out. Name the exact job and the exact time window. Vague asks get vague replies.

🧾 Keep a running allergy list

Confirm it with the teacher at the start of the year and re-check before every food event. Nut-free is the baseline. Labels on everything.

⏱️ Over-plan the time

Fourth graders finish things faster than you think. Have one extra game in your back pocket for the last 10 minutes.

🤝 Make the teacher the hero

Run every plan by the teacher first, keep it inside school rules, and give them the final call on anything loud, messy, or sugary.

📸 One photographer, not twenty

Assign a volunteer to take photos and share them to the class app. Check the school photo policy first. Some kids are opted out.

🙏 Thank people by name

A specific thank-you ("Priya ran the laser maze like a pro") gets you volunteers next time. Templates are on the Messages page.
