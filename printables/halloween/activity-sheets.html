Detective

How to solve: read a clue, then put an ✗ in every box it rules out. When a row has only one box left, that's the answer. Put a ✓ there and ✗ the rest of its column.

🎃 Who ate what at the monster party?

Four monsters each ate one snack: popcorn, pretzels, a red apple, or a cupcake with orange frosting. Nobody shared.

  1. The Vampire only eats red things.
  2. The Werewolf ate something crunchy, and it was not the popcorn.
  3. The Witch can't stand frosting.

🐈‍⬛ Three witches, three cats

Agatha, Belinda and Cora each have one cat: a black one, an orange one, and a gray one.

  1. Agatha's cat is not black and not gray.
  2. Cora is allergic to orange fur, and her cat is not black.
