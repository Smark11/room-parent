How the evidence adds up

Rule of the Bureau: a clue never says who did it. It says who didn't. Each station produces one fact; the squad checks it against the suspects' own statement forms and crosses off whoever doesn't match. Four stations, four suspects, four cross-offs. Whoever is left wasn't on the list.

  2. The Detective produces the fifth statement form, the Chief's, "filled out for completeness," and pins it to the board.
  3. The squad checks it line by line: size 9 ✓, classroom all afternoon ✓, orange sweater ✓, "Don't be ridiculous" crossed out, "Fine. Love them." ✓
