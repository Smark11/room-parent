Ghostbuster Lab

Word slips for the ghosts

Five words make the sentence. One set of five per squad, plus a spare set. Cut on the dashed lines, laminate or cover with clear tape, roll each slip tight, and bury one inside each baking-soda ghost. The tiny number in the corner tells you which set it belongs to, not the word order.

Baking-soda ghosts (makes 25)

  1. In a bowl, mix 2 boxes of baking soda with water, a tablespoon at a time, until it packs like damp sand (about 6 to 8 tablespoons).
  2. Pack a golf-ball amount into your palm, push a rolled slip into the middle, and shape a 3-inch ghost with a rounded head. Press two googly eyes in.
  3. Set each ghost in a muffin tin. Dry overnight, uncovered.
  4. Transport in the muffin tins. They crumble if they roll around.

At the station

  • Five ghosts in the bin, one per detective. Each detective gets a syringe of green vinegar (white vinegar + a few drops of green food coloring).
  • Bust the ghost until it fizzes apart and the slip appears. Sleeves up.
  • Squad lays out its five words: SEEN IN HALL AT 2:15. Write it in the case file.
  • Between squads: scoop out the fizz, set five fresh ghosts. The residue is harmless.
