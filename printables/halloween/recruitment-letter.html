Test 4 · The room test

Do not look up. Seriously. Eyes on this page.

A detective notices everything, even things nobody asked about. Answer from memory. Then look up and check.

  1. How many windows are in this room?
  2. What color is the classroom door?
  3. Is there a clock? What time does it say?
  4. Name something on the Chief's desk.
  5. What is on the board right now?
  6. Shoes on the person to your left: what color?
6 right: Eagle Eye.4–5: Sharp.2–3: Promising.0–1: You would walk past a dinosaur.
