Confidential
Room 12 Detective BureauFor: only

Detective,

You have been recruited by the Room 12 Detective Bureau.

Report to the Bureau on Friday, October 30, at 1:30 PM.

There has been an incident. We can't say more in writing. What we can say: there are suspects. There is evidence. And we need you.

Your training will cover footprint analysis, chemical testing, code-breaking, witness interviews, and one task we would rather not describe. It involves toilet paper.

Dress code: anything you don't mind getting wrapped in toilet paper. Costumes are welcome and not required. Every detective receives a badge at the door.

Bring: the enclosed Detective ID, filled in.

Tell: your family. No one else. Especially not older siblings. They talk.

— The Detective

No food is required for the party to work; if snacks are approved, they'll be store-bought, sealed, and nut-free. Costumes are optional.
