Detective's tip

Line the zero on the ruler up with the back of the heel. Read the number at the very tip of the toe. If your squad gets two different numbers, measure once more, together, and agree on one before you look at the chart.

This is the Bureau's own chart, simplified so every centimeter maps to one size. Real shoe charts are messier; nobody at this party will mind.

Powder cup labels

Cut out and tape to four cups. Three are the known powders; one is the "scene powder" and is secretly baking soda too. Only baking soda fizzes with vinegar, so the scene powder matches, which is the key to Station 2.

Running the powder test

  1. A pinch of each known powder on the tray, in a row, labels facing the squad.
  2. One pipette of vinegar on each. Which fizzes? (Baking soda.)
  3. Now a pinch of the EVIDENCE powder. One pipette. It fizzes. "So the powder at the scene was baking soda. Interesting. Remember that at Station 2."
  4. Pipettes, not pouring. Fizz happens on the tray only. Wipe between squads.

Fast squad bonus

Lift a fingerprint: rub a pencil on paper to make graphite dust, press a finger in it, press the finger onto clear tape, stick the tape to white paper. Instant evidence.
