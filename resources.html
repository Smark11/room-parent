Field trips

Chaperone game plan

The teacher owns the trip. The room parent's job is to make sure the chaperones show up, know the plan, and nobody is counting heads alone.

🧭 Room parent timeline

🎒 Chaperone pack list

  • Group list with every kid's name, any medical notes, and the teacher's cell
  • Phone fully charged, plus a battery pack
  • Small first-aid kit, tissues, wet wipes, hand sanitizer
  • Water, a snack for yourself, and one spare snack (nut-free) for the kid who forgot lunch
  • Comfortable shoes. You will walk more than you expect.
  • A bright hat or shirt so your group can find you
  • Sharpie and masking tape for emergency name tags

🔢 Chaperone rules of thumb

  • Count heads constantly. Before you move, after you move, after every bathroom stop. Say the number out loud.
  • Your group, your eyes. No phone scrolling. Photos of your own group only, per school policy.
  • Bathroom in pairs, and an adult waits outside.
  • No side trips. The gift shop is not on the itinerary unless the teacher says so.
  • Behavior issues go to the teacher. You redirect, you don't discipline.
  • Siblings stay home. Chaperones chaperone.

Sign-ups

Sign-up templates

Paste these into SignUpGenius, a Google Form, or the class app. Specific slots with exact times and quantities get filled. "Anyone want to help?" does not.

Three rules for sign-ups that fill: 1) name the job and the time window, 2) keep the list short enough to feel finishable, 3) close with a date, then send one reminder two days before that date.
